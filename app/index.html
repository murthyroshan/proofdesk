02 · How it works

Four steps, every one of them visible.

01Upload

The IQAC drops a metric's claimed number and its proofs: PDFs, phone photos, scans. Departments can send theirs through a link that opens from WhatsApp.

02Screen on-device

Every page is read on the college's own machine. Student phone numbers, roll numbers and lists of names are caught there and never sent to the cloud.

03Verify with two models

Gemini reads each proof and cites the exact line. Code checks the academic year, duplicates and who it was for. Gemma gives a second opinion; disagreement goes to a human.

04Pre-audit

Claimed vs proven, every flag with its evidence, a corrected claim and a printable report. AI flags; the IQAC decides.

03 · Privacy by design

Student data stays on the college's machine.

In real published accreditation evidence we found student phone and roll numbers on scanned, sideways and handwritten pages. Our first screen missed some, so ProofDesk is fail-closed: anything that looks like a list of people never leaves the machine.
